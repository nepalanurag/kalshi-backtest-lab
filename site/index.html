Kalshi Backtest Lab

A production-grade market data pipeline and a statistically rigorous backtesting lab for 15-minute BTC binary options.

What I built

Two portfolio pieces sharing one spine: a data pipeline that collects settled Kalshi markets, trade prints, and BTC spot into a BigQuery warehouse (130M trades, schema validation, quality checks, idempotent loads) with tiered storage — recent data in BigQuery native tables, older months archived to Drive as Parquet and queried via BigQuery external tables — and a backtesting lab with an event-driven engine, exact Kalshi fees, five pre-registered strategy arms, and the full multiple-testing arsenal.

Cost guardrails

BigQuery runs under a $1 billing budget with a kill-switch, plus application-level quotas enforced in code: every query dry-runs first and is refused if it would scan over 10GB, with a 100GB/day cap. The 10GB BigQuery free storage tier is the trigger for archiving old months to Drive.

Pre-registration

All five strategy arms and the primary comparison (Arm B vs Arm A) were frozen in METHODOLOGY.md before any backtest ran. Timestamp discipline is enforced by the engine: signals see only data strictly before the decision time.

Venue mechanics modeled exactly

Taker fee = ceil(0.07 × contracts × P × (1−P)) per order, verified against Kalshi's published table. Fills proxied by trade prints (historical quotes are unavailable via the public API) — documented as a limitation, not hidden.
